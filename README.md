# The Unclaimed Self | Shadow &amp; Authenticity

A free tool for shadow work, built on Carl Jung's idea that we all hand over
parts of ourselves in order to belong. It runs entirely on your own computer.

**[Download the latest build](../../releases/latest)** &middot; Windows 10 and 11

There is no Mac or Linux version.

---

## What it does

Sixty five statements, answered one at a time. Then a pass called A Closer Look,
which names the patterns your answers raised and asks a few more questions about
each, once regarding your personal life and once regarding your working life.
Then it asks when the pattern started, and for one specific memory. That last
part is what turns a general description into yours.

What comes out is one written report: what the pattern is in plain language, the
answers it was drawn from so you can disagree with it, what it protects you
from, and what it costs you. It exports as a PDF or as plain text.

After that there is a journal with prompts drawn from your own results, a
practice space, a timeline, and a reading list.

## What it is not

It is not therapy, counseling, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a
substitute for any of those. Its results are hypotheses to consider, not
findings about your health, character, or history.

It asks direct questions about your childhood and your relationships, and there
is nobody on the other end of it. **You must be 18 or older.**

If something it raises feels heavier than reflection, that is a sign to talk to
somebody. In the US, the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline is reachable by call or
text at 988.

## Your data

Nothing leaves your computer. There is no account, no server, and no sign-in to
anything. Nothing you answer or write is uploaded, and no usage is measured or
reported.

Your profile is encrypted with a password you choose. I cannot read it and I
cannot recover it, which is the same fact from both sides. Make a recovery key
when the program offers you one.

## Windows will warn you, and here is why

You will most likely see a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC" and
naming an unknown publisher. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

That is not a virus warning, and Windows has not found anything in the file. It
appears because the program is not code signed. A signing certificate is a paid
yearly subscription from a certificate authority, and what it buys is the
ability to show a verified company name instead of "unknown publisher". Every
unsigned program written by an individual gets this warning, including entirely
ordinary ones. It goes away when the certificate is bought, not when the
software becomes safer. I have not bought one yet.

If Defender or another scanner flags it too, that is the same story from a
different angle. Scanners treat new, unsigned programs with few downloads as
suspicious by default, because that also describes most genuinely bad ones.

If you would rather not run it on that basis, that is a reasonable position and
I would rather you said so than clicked through something you were uneasy
about. Ask and I will send you the source code to read first.

## Installing

Download the zip, unpack it, and keep the folder together. Run
`TheUnclaimedSelf-BETA.exe` from inside it. The program will not start without
the files beside it.

## This is a beta

It is unfinished and still changing, and how your answers are read can change
between versions. A report you generate today may not match one you generated
with an earlier build from the same answers. That is the program being
corrected rather than your answers being unstable. Export anything you want to
keep.

If something reads badly, drags, confuses you, or does not sound like you, that
is worth a line. There is a Feedback screen inside the program, or write to the
